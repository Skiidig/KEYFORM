### Non-Destructive Focus Architecture

- **No shared material mutation:** `ProductionScene.setFocusedAssemblyGroup(groupId)` keeps the focused group's meshes and `InstancedMesh` batches in their calibrated B3 materials (and preserves keycap legends intact) while swapping non-focused visible meshes onto a single lazy translucent ghost material (`KF01_AnatomyFocusGhost`, `opacity: 0.2`, `depthWrite: false`) and storing each original `{ material, castShadow }` in `focusOrigMaterials`.
- **Exact restoration:** `setFocusedAssemblyGroup(null)` (triggered by `Restore full view`, switching to `Assemble`, switching stations, or before `detachLegendMask`/`dispose`) restores exact original material references and shadow flags. Shared calibrated materials (`MAT_Case_Anodized`, `MAT_Keycap_Alpha`, `MAT_Keycap_Mod`, `MAT_Silicone`) are never mutated.
- **Occluded internal layer behavior:** Selecting any of the six internal layers while in assembled mode automatically transitions back to exploded inspection (`exploded = true`, `layerFocused = true`) so the UI never implies an occluded internal layer is visibly selected.
- **Interaction & continuity:** List buttons, `← Previous` / `Next →`, `Assemble` | `Explode`, and `Focus layer` / `Restore full view` remain fully synchronized across both static-fallback and interactive-3D modes. User manual orbit is preserved across layer selection; `Reset view` restores canonical framing; zero idle rendering is maintained.

## 2. Switch Lab — Standalone Hero Switch Presentation & Real-Time Actuation

### Verified GLB Geometry & Transform Contract (`kf01_hero_switch_b21.reference.glb`)

Inspected directly from the production standalone switch GLB (never modified):

- **Stationary lower body & guides:** Lower housing rim sits at `Y = 0.00489 m`; `HERO_MX_MoldedStemGuides` (`t = [0, 0, 0]`, `Y ∈ [0.00064, 0.00435] m`) are internal lower-housing guide rails and are **explicitly excluded** from stem travel (`guidesDeltaY === 0` at all poses and travel depths).
- **Presentation toggle (`Cutaway` | `Assembled`):**
  - `Cutaway` (default on activation): preserves the approved editorial exploded inspection pose (`upper t.y = 0.00980 m`, `stem t.y = 0.01820 m`, spring suspended to reveal internal geometry).
  - `Assembled`: deterministically seats the 3 upper housing nodes (`HERO_MX_PolycarbonateUpper`, `HERO_MX_UpperCornerHooks`, `HERO_MX_UpperReinforcingRibs`) to `t.y = 0.00489 m`, seats the 3 moving stem nodes (`HERO_MX_POMCrossStem`, `HERO_MX_POMSliderRunners`, `HERO_MX_StemCenterPost`) to rest `t.y = 0.00850 m`, and anchors `HERO_MX_VariablePitchSpring` base at `Y = 0.00100 m`.
- **Real-time stem actuation along local `-Y`:**
  - Pressing while in `Cutaway` transitions cleanly to `Assembled` so actuation never animates floating cutaway parts.
  - Moves **only** the 3 stem nodes along local `-Y` by each variant's nominal total travel (`linear45`: `4.0 mm`, `tactile58`: `3.8 mm`, `silent50`: `3.7 mm` — distinct from the installed keyboard's validated `4.0 mm` travel) while compressing `HERO_MX_VariablePitchSpring` (`scaleY = max(0.35, 1 - pressM / 0.00548)` with base pinned at `Y = 0.00100 m`).
  - Phase completion snaps deterministically (`heroPhase: 'idle' | 'pressing' | 'bottomed' | 'releasing'`) so both visual bottom-out/top-out and audio triggers fire cleanly and accumulate `0` transform drift across repeated cycles (`< 1e-6 m`).
  - Full release safety handles `pointerup`, `pointercancel`, `lostpointercapture`, keyboard `Space`/`Enter` up, window `blur`, `visibilitychange`, variant switch mid-press, 3D deactivation, and route unmount.
  - `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` snaps cleanly between rest and bottom-out with equivalent functionality and zero continuous idle loop.

## 3. Recorded Audio V2 Migration & Shared Lazy Engine

### Migrated Assets & Provenance

- Copied all **18 unmodified CC0-1.0 WAV files** (`341,600 B`, `44.1 kHz` mono 16-bit PCM) plus cleaned portable `manifest.json` (lab-local `sourceFile` stripped) and `SHA256SUMS` from read-only `D:\Assets\KEYFORM\audio-lab-v2\public\audio\` into `public/audio/kf01-v2/`.
- Provenance and attribution documented in [AUDIO_SOURCES.md](AUDIO_SOURCES.md) and [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- Static server (`tests/scripts/serve-dist.mjs`) serves `.wav` as `audio/wav` with immutable caching under `/audio/kf01-v2/`.
- Hosted verifier (`tests/scripts/verify-assets.mjs`) validates all `102` production assets (`83` existing untouched + `19` audio files) for SHA-256, byte size, MIME, `CC0-1.0` licence metadata, `RIFF`/`WAVE` 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit PCM headers, and `341,600 B` payload total.

### Shared Engine Architecture (`src/audio/`)

- Framework-independent and Three.js-independent (`src/audio/types.ts`, `src/audio/sampleProvider.ts`, `src/audio/engine.ts`, `src/audio/index.ts`), lazy-loaded through `src/audio/SwitchLabAudioPanel.tsx` (`4,182 B` gzip-9) **only** after explicit user click on `Enable sound`.
- **Autoplay & privacy safety:** `AudioContext` is created and resumed synchronously inside the `Enable sound` click gesture and handed to `MechanicalAudioEngine`. Zero `AudioContext` and zero `/audio/` requests occur on initial load or 3D activation.
- **Recorded-only fidelity:** Zero procedural DSP fallback. `RecordedSampleProvider` verifies manifest/file byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes (reusing `src/runtime/lib/digest.ts`), decodes all 18 buffers once on load, and serves synchronous round-robin `AudioBuffer` lookups with zero fetch/decode on playback.
- **Voice & lifecycle bounds:** `16`-voice cap with oldest-voice stealing, releasing-voice cap, `RECORDED_GAIN = 0.6`, `tanh(x * 1.15)` WaveShaper limiter, `lifecycleGen` cancellation guard preventing stale ready state on rapid `enable`/`disable`, and graceful silent fallback on 404/network error.
- **Synchronized playback:** Fires downstroke at `bottomed` and upstroke at `idle` after a completed bottom-out; partial presses released before bottom-out do not emit spurious audio.
- **Honest disclosures:** Discloses that recordings are representative CC0 samples (not physical KF—01 measurements), that `Silent 50` is a provisional dampened profile, and that release samples are shared across profiles.
