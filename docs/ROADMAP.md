## Engineering and release performance gates

- The historical single-payload goals of ≤ 2.5 MB and ≤ 40 draws remain optimization references. For integration, use B3's observed engineering reference of 4,490,660 B for exterior plus internal, approximately 56 exterior and 101 exploded draws, and the B3 review ceilings of 65 Balanced exterior, 75 Standard exterior, and 120 exploded draws.
- P1 measures the native lazy WebGL chunk against the historical ≤ 220 kB gzip goal. B3's approximately 372.50 kB gzip Inspector includes tooling. The visitor-entry goal remains ≤ 90 kB gzip. See the measured P1 evidence in the [handoff](HANDOFF.md).
- Release gates remain open for clean RTX 4060 timing, physical mobile and integrated-GPU testing, actual interaction frame times, the 60 fps desktop and ≥ 30 fps mobile/iGPU targets, and any accepted budget exceptions. Preserve the Hero LCP < 2.2 s, CLS < 0.05, INP < 100 ms, and accessibility targets in [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md). B3 lab timings came from contaminated runs with no usable GPU execution-timer medians.
